# Seasonal Adaptation Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHEN-F-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHEN-F-003 — Seasonality

## Definition

Audit functional retention under seasonal/extreme-weather/holiday patterns, including adaptive features across dimensions such as light, temperature, humidity, and holiday distribution

## Measurement

Seasonal scenario checklist (extreme weather/light cycles/holiday patterns) × function-retention audit; light-sensitive populations referenced against light requirements from SAD research

## Data sources

- Scenario audit
- Seasonal usage data

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Core functions do not fail under extreme weather/temperature (waterproofing/temperature-tolerance specs met) |
| Good | Mode adaptation to light/temperature changes (dark in winter/high contrast in summer/anti-glare) |
| Excellent | Automatic switching between holiday modes and seasonal content |

**Unit**: Scenario coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial
- architecture
- fashion

## Limitations

SAD (Rosenthal 1984) provides a reasonable basis for the lighting-mood association; seasonal operations strategy falls within business practice.
