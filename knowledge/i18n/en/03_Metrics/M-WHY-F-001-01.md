# Inclusive ROI Framework

> Metric ID: `M-WHY-F-001-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHY-F-001 — Cost Constraint

## Definition

Audit the quantified record of inclusion investment (compliance cost/development investment) and the benefit framework (market size/retrofit avoidance/brand value), against ROD 2024 market data

## Measurement

Inclusion investment ledger × benefit-dimension cross-reference (ROD 2024: global disability market $18.3T / US PWD disposable income $1.3T / 63% of population connected)

## Data sources

- Cost ledger
- Market data
- SROI analysis

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Inclusion investment has a dedicated record (not mixed into general development cost) |
| Good | A life-cycle cost comparison exists (upfront inclusive design vs later retrofit cost) |
| Excellent | Inclusion benefits are incorporated into the business case (market positioning/differentiation/procurement advantage) |

**Unit**: Ledger completeness %

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

ROD 2024 is a corporate report (survey with N=11,000+), not peer-reviewed research; use it as a business-case data source.
