# Non-Text Alternative Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-012-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-012 — Literacy

## Definition

Check whether core tasks have non-text alternative guidance such as diagrams/video/voice, and whether key copy uses controlled vocabulary

## Measurement

Audit of the task × alternative-channel matrix + spot checks on vocabulary complexity

## Data sources

- Content audit
- User testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Core tasks have ≥1 non-text guidance (pictorial or video) |
| Good | Key action words are controlled (within a common-vocabulary list) |
| Excellent | All tasks can be completed by voice guidance alone |

**Unit**: Task coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial
- service
