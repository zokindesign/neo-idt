# Aesthetic Satisfaction Rating

> Metric ID: `M-WHICH-F-005-02` · Category: **User testing** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHICH-F-005 — Aesthetic Style Preference

## Definition

Representative users' subjective satisfaction rating of the visual style — an application of the ISO 9241-11 satisfaction measure (preferences are neither right nor wrong; what is measured is the target population's acceptance)

## Measurement

Invite ≥8 representative users to fill in a 3-item satisfaction questionnaire (1-5 scale) after a real task, comparing before and after the redesign

## Data sources

- Satisfaction questionnaire
- Post-task interviews

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Mean ≥3.5/5 |
| Good | ≥4.0/5 |
| Excellent | ≥4.3/5 and recommendation intent is non-negative |

**Unit**: Satisfaction score (1-5)

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Aesthetic preferences vary widely across cultures; report distributions and between-group differences, not just means.

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-ISO-9241-11`
