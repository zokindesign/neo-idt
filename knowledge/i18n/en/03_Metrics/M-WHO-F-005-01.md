# Emotional Experience Three-Level Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-005-01` · Category: **User testing** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-005 — Emotion & Motivation

## Definition

Audit emotional touchpoints based on Norman's three levels (visceral/behavioral/reflective) and measure users' emotional responses

## Measurement

Emotional touchpoint map × three-level classification audit + PANAS/SAM scale measurement + SDT three-needs satisfaction assessment

## Data sources

- User testing
- Rating scales
- Interviews

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | No frustration-inducing points in key flows (PANAS negative score no higher than the baseline) |
| Good | Positive design at key touchpoints (sense of achievement/trust) with explicit support for the three SDT needs |
| Excellent | Empowerment moment design (perceptible progress/mastery) + peak-end rule optimization |

**Unit**: Scale score / touchpoint coverage %

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

PANAS/SAM/SDT are all validated scales; threshold benchmarks require norms established per product domain.
