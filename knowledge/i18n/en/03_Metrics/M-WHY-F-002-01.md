# Inclusive Market Positioning Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHY-F-002-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHY-F-002 — Market Positioning

## Definition

Audit the explicitness of the inclusive value proposition in product positioning (target-population coverage statement/differentiation statement/accessibility capability disclosure)

## Measurement

Positioning document × inclusive value-proposition checklist; assess the completeness of the target-population definition against ROD 2024's 63% population-connection data

## Data sources

- Marketing-document audit
- Competitor comparison

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Target-population definition does not exclude reasonably reachable groups (no "limited to"-style exclusion statements) |
| Good | Inclusive capability is explicit in the value proposition (e.g. accessibility qualification/age-friendly certification) |
| Excellent | Inclusion serves as a core differentiating selling point and is backed by procurement qualifications |

**Unit**: Check-item pass rate %

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Market-positioning audits examine strategy documents, not user outcomes; use alongside M-WHY-F-001-01.
