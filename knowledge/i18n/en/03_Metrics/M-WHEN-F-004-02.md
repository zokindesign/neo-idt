# Temporal Demand Score (NASA-TLX)

> Metric ID: `M-WHEN-F-004-02` · Category: **User testing** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHEN-F-004 — Time Pressure

## Definition

Use the NASA-TLX Temporal Demand subdimension to measure the subjective load of task time pressure — this scale's six dimensions include Temporal Demand, 0-20 points (Hart & Staveland 1988)

## Measurement

Invite ≥8 representative users before and after the redesign to complete the same core task and fill in NASA-TLX, comparing the means on the Temporal Demand dimension

## Data sources

- NASA-TLX questionnaire
- Task-completion records

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Mean temporal demand ≤15/20 |
| Good | ≤10/20 |
| Excellent | ≤6/20 and redesign reduction ≥30% |

**Unit**: TLX temporal demand score (0-20)

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Subjective scales are affected by individual differences; small samples must report dispersion, not just the mean.
