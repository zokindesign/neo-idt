# Spatial Accessibility Compliance Rate

> Metric ID: `M-WHERE-F-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **A — mandatory standard clause** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHERE-F-003 — Spatial Accessibility

## Definition

Measure item by item against GB 55019 mandatory clauses (clear passage width ≥900mm, ramps, grab bars, etc.) and ISO 21542 circulation clauses

## Measurement

On-site measurement + checklist (GB 55019 ch.2 circulation facilities / GB 50763 / ISO 21542:2021 cl.8 internal circulation and cl.9 building elements)

## Data sources

- Field measurement
- Checklist

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | 100% compliance with GB 55019 mandatory clauses (regulatory red line) |
| Good | ≥ 80% compliance with ISO 21542 recommended clauses |
| Excellent | Full route traversal in an on-site wheelchair walkthrough (including emergency evacuation paths) |

**Unit**: Clause pass rate %

## Applicable domains

- architecture

## Limitations

GB 55019 is a full-text mandatory code, applicable to new-build, renovation, and expansion projects.

- `STD-GB-55019:2`
- `STD-GB-50763:3`
- `STD-ISO-21542:8`
