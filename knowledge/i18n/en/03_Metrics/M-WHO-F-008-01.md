# Cultural Localization Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-008-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-008 — Cultural Background

## Definition

Conduct a cross-cultural audit of icon metaphors, color symbolism, name/date/address formats, and reading direction

## Measurement

Item-by-item audit against the target-culture checklist + validation with users from ≥2 cultural backgrounds

## Data sources

- Localization checklist
- Cultural-background user interviews

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | No offensive symbols and support for localized formats (name/date/address) |
| Good | Icons and metaphors validated with users from ≥2 cultural backgrounds |
| Excellent | Support for RTL layout and all target-market languages |

**Unit**: Audit-item pass rate %

- universal
