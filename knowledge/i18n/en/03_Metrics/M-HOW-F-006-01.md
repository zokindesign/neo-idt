# Assistive Technology Interoperability

> Metric ID: `M-HOW-F-006-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: HOW-F-006 — Assistive Technology Compatibility

## Definition

Measured pass rate for completing core tasks with a screen reader / switch control / voice control

## Measurement

Testing with mainstream AT combinations (WCAG 4.1.2 Name/Role/Value check + full core-task operation via AT)

## Data sources

- Direct AT testing
- Automated scanning

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Zero WCAG 4.1.2 failures and core tasks completable with a screen reader (Level A baseline) |
| Good | Core-task success rate ≥ 90% with mainstream AT combinations |
| Excellent | Passes across all AT combinations + rare AT (switch/eye control) |

**Unit**: Task success rate %

## Applicable domains

- digital

## Limitations

Combinatorial explosion of AT combinations: prioritize the mainstream combinations per the WebAIM survey.

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:4.1.2`
- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:4.1.3`
