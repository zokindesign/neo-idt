# Data Collection Minimisation

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-014-02` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **A — mandatory standard clause** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-014 — Privacy & Security Concerns

## Definition

Audit whether the personal information the product collects aligns with core-function necessity — an application of GDPR Article 5(1)(c) data minimisation (adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary) and the PIPL minimum-necessity principle

## Measurement

Inventory collected fields form by form/permission by permission → annotate each field's functional necessity → compute the proportion of non-essential collected fields and verify purpose statements

## Data sources

- Form and permission checklist
- Functional requirements documents
- Privacy-policy cross-check

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | No required personal information unrelated to core functionality |
| Good | 100% of non-required collected fields have a purpose statement |
| Excellent | Zero non-essential collection and both export and deletion channels are provided |

**Unit**: Non-essential collected-field percentage

## Applicable domains

- digital
- service

## Limitations

The judgment of "necessary" depends on how functional boundaries are defined and must be confirmed jointly by product and legal.

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-GDPR`
- `STD-PIPL`
