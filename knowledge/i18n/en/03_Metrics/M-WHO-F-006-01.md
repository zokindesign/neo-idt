# Sensory Intensity Adjustability

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-006-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-006 — Sensory Preference

## Definition

Audit the intensity-adjustment capability of each sensory channel (visual/auditory/tactile), setting adjustment ranges with reference to the sensitivity distribution of the HSP scale

## Measurement

Audit of the sensory-channel × adjustable-property matrix (brightness/color temperature/volume/vibration/motion intensity), setting adjustment ranges against the HSP sensitivity distribution

## Data sources

- Feature audit
- User testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Intensity of primary sensory channels adjustable (brightness/volume/vibration) |
| Good | Motion can be disabled (responds to prefers-reduced-motion) + non-stimulating mode (low-sensory mode) |
| Excellent | Continuously adjustable across all channels + contextual sensory-profile memory |

**Unit**: Channel adjustability %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial

## Limitations

The Aron & Aron 1997 HSP scale supports the existence of a sensitivity distribution; quantitative thresholds for the modulation range still require user-calibration research.
