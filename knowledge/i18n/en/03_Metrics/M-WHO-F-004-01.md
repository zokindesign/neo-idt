# Multimodal Presentation Completeness

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-004-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **D — project inference** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-004 — Learning Style

## Definition

Audit the presentation of key content through more than two channels (text and graphics + video + interactive walkthrough), positioning the design response as multimodal presentation rather than style adaptation per the conclusions of Pashler 2008

## Measurement

Key content × presentation modality matrix audit (whether content can be accessed via ≥ 2 channels)

## Data sources

- Content audit
- User testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Key operation guidance available in ≥ 2 modalities (e.g., text + graphics and video) |
| Good | User-selectable default learning/help modality |
| Excellent | All content in ≥ 2 modalities with user-controlled pacing (repeat/fast-forward/slow playback) |

**Unit**: Content dual-modality coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial
- service

## Limitations

Pashler et al. 2008 rejects the evidence base for "learning-style matching" — this metric measures "multimodal presentation" (which has consensus support), not "style matching".
