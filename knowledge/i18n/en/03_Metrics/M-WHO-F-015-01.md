# Gender Inclusivity Completeness

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-015-01` · Category: **User testing** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-015 — Gender & Gender Expression

## Definition

Audit the neutrality of gender fields, the configurability of salutations/pronouns, and the neutrality of sizing systems; invite transgender/non-binary users to validate

## Measurement

Audit of gender-related touchpoint checklist (forms/salutations/spaces/sizing) + live testing with transgender and non-binary users (Haimson 2016/2023 methodology: identity-change scenarios and community-participatory design)

## Data sources

- UI audit
- Community user testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Gender field is optional and includes non-binary/prefer-not-to-say options; pronouns are configurable |
| Good | A path exists to change historical data (records can be updated after identity transition) |
| Excellent | All-gender space options + sizing based on body measurements rather than gender binary |

**Unit**: Touchpoint coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- architecture
- fashion

## Limitations

In some jurisdictions gender-field options are a compliance requirement (e.g. Argentina's Gender Identity Law) — jurisdictions differ widely; confirm the target market before auditing.
