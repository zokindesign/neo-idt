# Neurodiversity Feature Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-009-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-009 — Neurodiversity

## Definition

Check neurodiversity-friendly features such as reduced motion, predictable layout, plain language, content chunking, and no-time-pressure defaults

## Measurement

Audit against a neurodiversity design checklist (motion toggle/layout consistency/language plainness/content chunking/default time limits), including verification of prefers-reduced-motion response

## Data sources

- Feature checklist
- Code/UI audit

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | A reduce-motion option is provided and layout stays predictable |
| Good | Plain language and content chunking meet the bar |
| Excellent | Sensory intensity fully adjustable and a focus mode is provided |

**Unit**: Feature coverage %
