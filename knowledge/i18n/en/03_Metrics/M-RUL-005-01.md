# Ethics Review Mechanism Completeness

> Metric ID: `M-RUL-005-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: RUL-005 — Ethics Review

## Definition

Audit the ethics-review mechanism per the three Belmont principles and the IEEE EAD framework: informed-consent flow, risk-benefit assessment, fairness audit, and grievance/appeal channel

## Measurement

Ethics checklist audit (Belmont: respect [autonomy and protection] → informed consent, beneficence → risk-benefit assessment, justice → fair selection; IEEE EAD: transparency/accountability/explainability) + spot checks of user research protocols

## Data sources

- Ethics checklist
- Research protocol
- Complaint records

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Human-subject research has an informed consent process and IRB/ethics committee review on record (each of the three Belmont principles verifiable) |
| Good | Algorithmic decisions have fairness audit records + self-check cleared of dark patterns + ethics feedback channel available |
| Excellent | Ethics assessment front-loaded into the design phase (design ethics workshops) + AI systems aligned with the IEEE EAD eight-principle framework |

**Unit**: Check-item coverage %

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Belmont addresses human-subjects research ethics and IEEE EAD addresses AI systems — the two are complementary frameworks; the depth of ethics review depends on product category (medical/AI/social products need domain-specific refinement).
