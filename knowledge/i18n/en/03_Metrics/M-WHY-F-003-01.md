# Dark Pattern Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHY-F-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHY-F-003 — Business Model

## Definition

Audit manipulative design according to the Mathur et al. 2019 taxonomy (countdown pressure/fake inventory/hidden cancellation/forced bundling)

## Measurement

Screen-by-screen audit of key flows against the dark-pattern checklist (7 major categories, 15 types); measure the symmetry between cancellation-flow steps and registration-flow steps

## Data sources

- UI audit
- Direct process testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Cancellation steps ≤ registration steps and no hidden cancellation (EU consumer-protection directive practice) |
| Good | No deceptive patterns from the Mathur taxonomy across the entire flow |
| Excellent | Key decisions have a cooling-off period/undo mechanism |

**Unit**: Dark-pattern count (target 0)

## Applicable domains

- digital

## Limitations

The Mathur et al. 2019 taxonomy is the source of the audit framework (empirical evidence from 11K sites); regulatory thresholds are adjusted per the target market's consumer-protection law.
