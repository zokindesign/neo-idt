# Battery-Life Stress Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-HOW-F-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: HOW-F-003 — Power & Battery Life

## Definition

Audit coping features for the high-pressure scenarios identified by Rahmati & Zhong's 2009 human-battery interaction study (low-battery anxiety / no charging access / extended time away from power)

## Measurement

High-stress scenario checklist audit (power-saving mode/low-power optimization/battery prediction/multiple charging methods), against behavioral patterns from human-battery interaction research

## Data sources

- Feature checklist
- Direct measurement

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Power-saving mode provided and battery status viewable |
| Good | Remaining-usage-time prediction and low-power strategy |
| Excellent | Redundant multiple charging methods (wired/wireless/external) and scenario-adaptive power reduction |

**Unit**: Scenario coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial

## Limitations

Rahmati & Zhong 2009 studied mobile-phone scenarios; wearable/medical-device scenarios need adjustment by product category.
