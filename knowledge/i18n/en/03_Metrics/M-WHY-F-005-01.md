# Stakeholder Conflict Resolution

> Metric ID: `M-WHY-F-005-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHY-F-005 — Stakeholder Impact

## Definition

According to the Freeman 1984 stakeholder identification framework, audit the explicit records and resolution strategies for conflicts among multiple parties' needs

## Measurement

Audit of stakeholder map → needs-conflict register → resolution-strategy records (decisions traceable)

## Data sources

- Design-document audit
- Interview records

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Conflicts between core users' and payers' needs are explicitly recorded |
| Good | Each conflict has a basis for the ruling and a statement of affected parties |
| Excellent | Vulnerable stakeholders (vulnerable groups among end users) have an advocacy mechanism |

**Unit**: Conflict-record completeness %

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Freeman 1984 is a management framework, not a quantitative standard; the audit examines process records, not outcomes.
