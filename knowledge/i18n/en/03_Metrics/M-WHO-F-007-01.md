# First-Time Novice Success Rate

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-007-01` · Category: **User testing** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-007 — Technical Literacy

## Definition

Recruit low-tech-literacy users (e.g., older adults using a smart device for the first time) to complete core tasks without assistance

## Measurement

Unassisted usability test, recording first-attempt task success rate, number of help requests and completion time

## Data sources

- Usability testing
- Participant observation

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Core-task unassisted success rate ≥ 80% |
| Good | ≥ 90% and average help requests ≤ 1 |
| Excellent | ≥ 95% and completion time ≤ expert-user P95 |

**Unit**: Success rate %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial

## Limitations

The SUS scale can serve as a supplementary measure (industry mean of 68 as reference, per Sauro's meta-analysis).
