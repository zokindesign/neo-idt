# Health Condition Accommodation Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-003 — Health Status

## Definition

Audit design completeness of accommodation for common health-impact categories (sensory/motor/chronic pain/fatigue/fluctuation during medication)

## Measurement

Health-impact category × accommodation measure matrix audit (including WCAG AA baseline check)

## Data sources

- Accommodation checklist
- Expert audit

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | 100% accommodation for sensory and motor categories (WCAG AA baseline) |
| Good | Explicit design for common chronic-condition impacts (fatigue/pain/cognitive fluctuation) |
| Excellent | Dedicated safeguards for low-frequency high-risk conditions (e.g., photosensitive epilepsy) |

**Unit**: Category coverage %

## Applicable domains

- universal
