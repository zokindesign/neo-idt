# Style Choice & Consistency

> Metric ID: `M-WHICH-F-005-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHICH-F-005 — Aesthetic Style Preference

## Definition

Audit official themes/customization capabilities, and the stability of feature locations when switching styles (reducing relearning cost)

## Measurement

Theme and customization capability inventory audit + pre/post-switch feature-location consistency walkthrough

## Data sources

- UI audit
- User testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | ≥ 2 official themes provided with a universally usable default (highly readable neutral style) |
| Good | Key customization capabilities (font size/contrast/motion toggles) + feature locations unchanged after switching |
| Excellent | Style preferences remembered and synced across platforms |

**Unit**: Selection tier / consistency pass rate

## Applicable domains

- digital

## Limitations

Aesthetic-preference measurement is inherently subjective; Norman's three-level theory supports the affective dimension, but thresholds are design consensus.
