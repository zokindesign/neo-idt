# Cognitive Demand Exclusion Rate

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-010-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-010 — Cognitive Traits

## Definition

Using the cognitive dimensions of the Cambridge exclusion calculation (memory/attention/comprehension), rate task cognitive demands and estimate the cognitive exclusion rate

## Measurement

Rate task cognitive demands (count of simultaneously held items/number of steps/decision points) → compare against the capability distribution to estimate exclusion

## Data sources

- Task analysis
- Expert rating

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Core tasks require holding ≤ 4 items simultaneously and steps can be chunked |
| Good | Estimated cognitive exclusion ≤ 5% |
| Excellent | ≤ 2% and burden-reducing aids such as pre-fill/progress hints are provided |

**Unit**: Exclusion percentage / memory-item count

## Applicable domains

- digital
- service

## Limitations

The memory-item threshold is a consensus value; quantitative anchors need subsequent calibration with a NASA-TLX-like scale.
