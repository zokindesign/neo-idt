# Target Market Safety Certification Readiness

> Metric ID: `M-RUL-003-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: RUL-003 — Safety Certification Requirements

## Definition

Audit the product's clause-level readiness for mandatory safety certifications in the target market (CE/UL/FDA/MDR/ISO 26262, etc.)

## Measurement

Target market × certification scheme matrix audit: certification checklist confirmation → traceability of safety requirements in design documents → pre-compliance testing gap analysis

## Data sources

- Certification checklist
- Design-document audit
- Pre-test reports

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Mandatory certification checklist for the target markets confirmed and safety requirements traceable to design documents |
| Good | Hazard analysis and risk assessment documentation complete; pre-compliance testing passed |
| Excellent | Parallel multi-certification path (one design satisfies certifications for multiple markets) + certification maintenance process (re-audit tracking) |

**Unit**: Certification readiness rate %

## Applicable domains

- industrial
- digital

## Limitations

Certification schemes carry voluminous clauses; this metric audits readiness processes rather than certification itself — certification outcomes are issued by certification bodies; strongly regulated domains (medical/automotive) require review by jurisdiction specialists.

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-ISO-26262`
