# Data Subject Rights Response Compliance

> Metric ID: `M-RUL-002-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **A — mandatory standard clause** · Status: draft

Related factor: RUL-002 — Data Privacy Compliance

## Definition

Audit whether data-subject-rights processes (withdraw consent / access and copy / rectify and supplement / delete) meet the time-limit and convenience requirements of the target jurisdiction

## Measurement

Data rights process walkthrough × jurisdiction clause matrix (GDPR: withdrawal as easy as giving consent [Art.7(3)]/response within one month [Art.12(3)]/right to erasure [Art.17]; PIPL: convenient withdrawal [Article 15]/may not refuse service [Article 16]/notification [Article 17]/informed decision/access and copy/correction/erasure [Articles 44-47])

## Data sources

- Process audit
- Support-ticket logs
- Direct withdrawal-path testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | All statutory rights paths exist in the target jurisdictions and time limits are met (GDPR one-month response; PIPL convenient withdrawal) |
| Good | Consent withdrawal path reachable on the same page and level as giving consent (not buried in settings), with status tracking for rights requests |
| Excellent | Fully self-service exercise of rights + internal processing-time SLA stricter than legally required + automatic routing of multi-jurisdiction requests |

**Unit**: Clause coverage % / response timeliness

## Applicable domains

- digital
- service

## Limitations

This metric is a process-compliance audit, not legal advice; multi-jurisdiction products must layer jurisdiction clauses such as CCPA/CPRA per launch market — currently the GDPR/PIPL dual jurisdictions are covered.
