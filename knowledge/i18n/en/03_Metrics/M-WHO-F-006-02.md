# Multisensory Redundancy Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-006-02` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **A — mandatory standard clause** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-006 — Sensory Preference

## Definition

Check whether information is conveyed through a single sensory channel alone — WCAG 1.4.1 (color must not be the only visual means) and 1.3.3 (instructions must not rely solely on sensory characteristics such as shape/color/position/sound)

## Measurement

Inventory information points in the interface and documentation that convey meaning by color/shape/position/sound alone → check each point for redundant encoding (text/icon/pattern/speech)

## Data sources

- UI inventory
- Information-point checklist check

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Zero information points conveyed by color alone |
| Good | Zero information points conveyed by any single sensory channel alone |
| Excellent | Key information has multimodal alternatives (visual + auditory/tactile) |

**Unit**: Non-compliant information-point count

## Applicable domains

- universal

## Limitations

Purely decorative differences are out of scope; equivalence of multimodal alternatives requires manual judgment.

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:1.4.1`
- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:1.3.3`
