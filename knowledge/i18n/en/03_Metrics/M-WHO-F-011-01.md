# Language Accessibility Audit

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-011-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-011 — Language

## Definition

Verify the page language declaration (lang), content reading level, and the accessibility of plain-language alternatives

## Measurement

lang attribute check (WCAG 3.1.1/3.1.2) + content reading-level measurement + target-language coverage checklist

## Data sources

- Code audit
- Content analysis

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | lang declaration is correct and target-market language coverage is 100% |
| Good | Core content reading level ≤ junior-high level |
| Excellent | A plain-language version and a glossary are provided |

**Unit**: Coverage / reading level

## Applicable domains

- digital
- service

## Limitations

Readability-level measurement tools must be selected by language (Chinese and English scales differ).

## Related standard clauses

- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:3.1.1`
- `STD-W3C-WCAG22:3.1.2`
