# Sustainability Metrics Coverage

> Metric ID: `M-WHY-F-004-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHY-F-004 — Sustainability & Environmental Goals

## Definition

Against the triple bottom line (Elkington 1997), audit the quantified targets and data disclosure of the environmental dimension

## Measurement

Four-part check of energy efficiency/lifespan/repairability/recyclability; for powered products, verify energy labels and carbon-footprint disclosure

## Data sources

- Product-specification audit
- Supply-chain data

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Complies with applicable environmental regulations (mandatory items of RoHS/energy-efficiency standards) |
| Good | Discloses energy-efficiency and repairability data (right-to-repair directive practice) |
| Excellent | Full life-cycle carbon-footprint disclosure and recycling channels |

**Unit**: Covered-item pass rate %

## Applicable domains

- industrial
- digital
- fashion

## Limitations

Elkington 1997's triple bottom line is the framework source; concrete thresholds follow product category and applicable regulation (e.g. EU ESPR).
