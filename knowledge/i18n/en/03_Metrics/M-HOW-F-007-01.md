# Output Channel Redundancy

> Metric ID: `M-HOW-F-007-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: HOW-F-007 — Input Output Capability

## Definition

Key information output does not rely on a single sensory channel (vision not reliant on color — WCAG 1.4.1; audio has captions/text alternatives)

## Measurement

Output event × channel matrix audit (WCAG 1.4.1 Use of Color, 1.2.2 Captions, 1.1.1 Non-text Content checks)

## Data sources

- Content audit
- AT testing

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Color is not the sole information carrier (WCAG 1.4.1 Level A) and media has alternatives (1.1.1/1.2.2 Level A) |
| Good | Dual-channel output covers all key information |
| Excellent | User-selectable preferred output channel with cross-channel synchronization |

**Unit**: Channel redundancy %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial

## Limitations

For hardware indicator-light output, an audit against ISO 9241-110 5.2 principles may substitute.
