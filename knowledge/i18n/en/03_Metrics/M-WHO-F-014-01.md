# Privacy Control Completeness

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-014-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-014 — Privacy & Security Concerns

## Definition

Audit permission granularity, data deletion/export entry points, guest mode and retention policy, and verify the response time for user rights requests

## Measurement

Privacy-control checklist audit + exercise of the rights-request process (within the scope of GDPR/PIPL application)

## Data sources

- Privacy policy
- Product audit
- Support-ticket data

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Permissions can be toggled individually and the account-deletion entry point is reachable (within the legally mandated response period) |
| Good | Data export and scenario-specific authorization are provided |
| Excellent | Guest/anonymous mode and local-processing options are provided |

**Unit**: Checklist pass rate %

## Applicable domains

- digital

## Limitations

Legal obligations depend on the target jurisdiction; thresholds must be adjusted per applicable regulations.
