# Demographic Representation Gap

> Metric ID: `M-WHO-F-002-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **C — expert consensus** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHO-F-002 — Demographics

## Definition

Compare the research sample and persona distribution against the target market's demographics (age group/gender/region) and calculate the representativeness deviation

## Measurement

Persona vs. target-population statistics comparison table + cross-dimension coverage check

## Data sources

- User-research samples
- Demographic data
- Persona documents

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Representation across all three dimensions of age/gender/region with no single-point persona |
| Good | Deviation from the target population ≤ 20% per dimension |
| Excellent | Deviation ≤ 10% with coverage of intersectional disadvantaged combinations (e.g., older adults × low tech literacy) |

**Unit**: Deviation percentage

## Applicable domains

- universal
