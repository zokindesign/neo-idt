# Economic Accessibility Tiers

> Metric ID: `M-WHERE-F-005-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: WHERE-F-005 — Economic Environment

## Definition

Audit free tiers/low-cost tiers and device and data cost optimization against the target market's income distribution and financial inclusion data

## Measurement

Pricing tiers × target-market income distribution comparison (World Bank income classification/poverty lines as reference); audit of data usage and legacy-device support

## Data sources

- Pricing audit
- Findex data
- User research

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | A free or low-cost access path exists (no paywall on core functions) |
| Good | Support for low-end devices and data-saving modes (offline/lite version) |
| Excellent | Tiered pricing by ability to pay (student/regional pricing/charitable licensing) |

**Unit**: Gradient coverage tiers

## Applicable domains

- digital
- service
- industrial

## Limitations

Findex 2021 is country-level financial-inclusion data; individual payment capacity requires supplementary research.
