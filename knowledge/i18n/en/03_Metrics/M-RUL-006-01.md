# Industry Access Standards Mapping

> Metric ID: `M-RUL-006-01` · Category: **Manual audit** · Evidence grade: **B — verified research** · Status: draft

Related factor: RUL-006 — Industry Specific Standards

## Definition

Audit the product's clause-level mapping to and compliance status with target-industry admission standards (HIPAA/PCI DSS/FERPA/DO-178C, etc.)

## Measurement

Target industry × access regulation matrix audit (healthcare HIPAA/finance PCI DSS/education FERPA/aviation DO-178C) → evidence mapping of clause requirements to design/process

## Data sources

- Industry-standards checklist
- Evidence-mapping documents

## Tiers

| Tier | Threshold |
|------|-----------|
| Minimum compliance | Target-industry access regulation checklist confirmed with a designated owner |
| Good | Each applicable clause has evidence mapping (design documents/test reports/process records) |
| Excellent | Industry regulation change-tracking mechanism + mapping reuse across multiple parallel regulations for cross-industry products |

**Unit**: Clause-mapping coverage %

## Applicable domains

- digital
- industrial
- service

## Limitations

Industry norms evolve with jurisdictions and sectors (e.g. PCI DSS version updates); the checklist must be reviewed annually and per the industry of deployment; HIPAA/PCI DSS clauses are not in this project's standards registry — register as needed.
