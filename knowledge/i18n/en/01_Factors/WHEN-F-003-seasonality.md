## Definition
The influence of seasonal climate change (temperature, humidity, daylight duration, holiday distribution, etc.) on the use of a product/service.

## Why It Matters
- User needs and environmental conditions differ greatly across seasons (e.g., sun protection in summer, warmth in winter)
- Holiday patterns change users' time availability and social settings
- Ignoring seasonality can leave features idle or make the experience uncomfortable

## Quantification Criteria
- **Climate parameters**: temperature range, rainfall, hours of sunshine
- **Seasonal usage rates**: fluctuation in daily actives/visits across seasons
- **Holiday effects**: usage peaks before and during holidays
- **Seasonal constraints**: number of days of extreme weather (heat waves, severe cold, rainy season)

## Design Guidelines
- Offer seasonal themes or interfaces (e.g., darker in winter, brighter in summer)
- Seasonal feature toggles (e.g., rainy-season reminders, insect protection)
- Adjust operational strategies (seasonal merchandise, seasonal content pushes)
- Consider climate effects on hardware (waterproofing, temperature tolerance)

## Cross-Domain Manifestations

| Domain | Adaptation Examples |
|------|----------|
| **Outdoor products** | UV index alerts, rain-gear carry reminders, anti-slip design |
| **Digital services** | Holiday theme skins, seasonal event pushes, seasonal content recommendations |
| **Fashion design** | Seasonal fabrics, detachable layers, breathability/warmth balance |
| **Architecture** | Shading, insulation, ventilation, rainwater harvesting |

## Related Standards
- No mandatory standard; refer to meteorological data and seasonal marketing best practices
