## Definition
Users' emotional states while using a product or service, their intrinsic drives, goals, and values, and their subjective experience of using it — including trust, belonging, achievement, frustration, anxiety, and other emotional factors.

## Why It Matters
Emotion and motivation directly determine whether users are willing to start using, keep using, and be satisfied with and loyal to a product. Even a functionally usable product gets abandoned when negative emotions (frustration, fear) take over. Considering emotion and motivation helps design products or services that are not just usable but pleasant, empowering, and aligned with users' values.

## Related Standards and References
- ISO 9241-210: human-centred design (covering user satisfaction and pleasure)
- User experience (UX) models: e.g., the joy-of-use model, the three levels of emotional design (visceral, behavioral, reflective)
- Self-Determination Theory: the three basic needs of intrinsic motivation (autonomy, competence, relatedness)
- Gamification design principles
- Research on brand emotional attachment

## Suggested Quantification Methods
- Affect measures: rating scales (e.g., PANAS — the Positive and Negative Affect Schedule; SAM — the Self-Assessment Manikin)
- Satisfaction scales: e.g., CSAT, NPS, and emotion-related items in the SUS (System Usability Scale)
- Motivation measures: intrinsic motivation scales (e.g., adapted versions such as the Sport Motivation Scale)
- Behavioral indicators: usage duration and frequency, voluntary referral rates, repurchase rates
- Applying depression and anxiety screening tools in specific contexts (e.g., anxiety when using medical devices)
- Data sources: user interviews, diary studies, experience sampling method (ESM), behavioral log analysis

## Manifestations Across Domains
- **Digital products**: achievement systems, positive feedback, less shame around errors, a sense of control, social-connection features
- **Industrial design**: comfort and pride during use, smoothness of operation, convenience of maintenance and repair
- **Architectural design**: the calm a space provides, belonging, safety, designs that spark creativity or social interaction
- **Interior design**: the relaxation furniture affords, emotional responses to color and material, a sense of flow through space
- **Graphic design**: emotional resonance of visual communication; how presentation shapes credibility and approachability
- **Fashion design**: self-image satisfaction after dressing, comfort and confidence, a medium for expressing identity

## Common Misconceptions and Blind Spots
- Focusing only on functional usability and ignoring the emotional experience
- Assuming all users share the same emotional triggers
- Over-relying on reward mechanics while neglecting the cultivation of intrinsic motivation
- Ignoring potential anxiety or trauma triggers in high-pressure or sensitive contexts
- Equating "delight" with gamified entertainment rather than context-appropriate positive feeling

## Design Recommendation Keywords
Emotional feedback, trust building, sense of empowerment, belonging, positive reinforcement, reduced anxiety, autonomy support, meaning, social connection, pleasantness
