## Definition
The urgency users feel under external time limits, and how well the time needed to complete a task matches the time they expected.

## Why It Matters
Time pressure causes:
- Increased cognitive load
- Reduced decision quality
- User anxiety and frustration
- Higher error rates

## Quantification Criteria
- **Time limits**: whether the task has a hard deadline
- **Response time requirements**: the response time window users expect (e.g., within 2 seconds)
- **Tolerance**: users' time tolerance thresholds for different tasks

## Design Guidelines
- Show remaining time or progress indicators
- Allow pausing/extending time (e.g., an extendable exam countdown)
- Provide express paths (alternative flows with fewer steps)
- Avoid unnecessary waiting and loading

## Cross-Domain Manifestations
- **Digital products**: loading animations, progress bars, allowing draft saving
- **Industrial design**: fast startup, one-touch completion of common tasks
- **Architecture**: clearly marked emergency evacuation routes
- **Service design**: express lane options, appointment time selection

## Related Standards
- Nielsen Norman Group: response time thresholds (0.1 s perceived as instant, 1 s keeps the flow, 10 s holds attention)
