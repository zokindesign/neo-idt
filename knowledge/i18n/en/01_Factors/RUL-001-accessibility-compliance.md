## Definition
The accessibility provisions within the laws and regulations that products and spaces must comply with.

## Why It Matters
- Violations can lead to lawsuits, fines, and product withdrawal
- They are entry barriers for certain markets
- They concern social responsibility and brand value

## Quantitative Criteria
- **Specific standards**: WCAG 2.1 AA, Section 508, EN 301 549, etc.
- **Coverage**: the proportion of success criteria satisfied
- **Self-assessment / third-party audit**: pass rate

## Design Guidelines
- Treat regulatory requirements as the minimum bar
- Keep records of compliance checks
- Resolve critical compliance items first
- Watch for regulatory updates and iterate accordingly

## Related Standards
- WCAG 2.1/2.2 (the global web standard)
- Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA, US)
- EN 301 549 (EU ICT products)
- GB/T 37668-2019 (China's internet content accessibility)
