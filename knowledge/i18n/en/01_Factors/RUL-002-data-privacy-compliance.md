## Definition
Compliance with privacy-related regulations across the entire data lifecycle: collection, storage, processing, and sharing.

## Why It Matters
- Severe penalties (up to 4% of global turnover under GDPR)
- Central to user trust
- Cross-border business must satisfy the requirements of different jurisdictions

## Quantitative Criteria
- **Consent rate**: the proportion of users who give valid consent
- **Data minimization**: whether the types of data collected exceed what is necessary
- **Retention period**: whether storage limits are defined and deletion is enforced
- **User rights response**: turnaround time for deletion, correction, and portability requests

## Design Guidelines
- Privacy by Design principles
- Clear privacy policy and usage instructions
- Provide a permissions management interface so users can withdraw consent at any time
- Capability to anonymize data

## Related Standards
- GDPR (EU)
- CCPA/CPRA (California)
- PIPL (China's Personal Information Protection Law)
- ISO/IEC 27701 (privacy information management)
