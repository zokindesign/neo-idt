## Basic Definition
Differences in how users' brains process information, including neurodevelopmental differences such as the autism spectrum, ADHD, dyslexia, and Tourette syndrome, as well as mental health states such as anxiety, depression, and PTSD.

## Why It Matters
- Neurodivergent people have different needs in perception, attention, social interaction, language, and learning
- Ignoring these differences leads to poor experiences, anxiety, and can even trigger adverse reactions
- Being inclusive of neurodiversity improves usability for all users (e.g., clearer instructions reduce cognitive load for everyone)

## Quantification Criteria
- **Attention sustainment**: average duration of single-task focus, recovery time after being interrupted
- **Sensory sensitivity**: sensitivity thresholds for light, sound, touch, etc. (measurable by scale)
- **Information processing speed**: reading speed, decision time, working memory capacity
- **Language processing**: reading comprehension difficulty, word/character recognition accuracy

## Design Guidelines
- Provide sensory regulation options (turn off motion, reduce brightness, lower sound)
- Avoid sudden changes (flashing, unexpected sounds)
- Provide clear, consistent navigation and feedback
- Let users control the pace (pause, replay, skip)
- Provide multi-channel information (text, icons, speech as options)

## Cross-Domain Manifestations
| Domain | Adaptive Design |
|------|------------|
| **Digital products** | Focus mode, reduced animation, simplified interfaces, gentler error messages |
| **Workspaces** | Noise-cancelling headphones, individual workstations, flexible working hours |
| **Architectural spaces** | Quiet retreat corners, clear signage, controllable lighting, reduced background noise |
| **Fashion design** | Tagless clothing, soft fabrics, non-restrictive cuts, predictable dressing and undressing steps |

## Related Standards
- WCAG 2.3: no flashing (seizures)
- ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) covers part of the neurodiversity scope
- Various national mental health and anti-discrimination regulations
