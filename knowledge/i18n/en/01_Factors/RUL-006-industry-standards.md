## Definition
Safety and quality norms specific to particular industries (healthcare, finance, education, aviation, etc.).

## Why It Matters
- Industry entry barriers
- Professional certification requirements
- Safety and quality standards

## Quantification Criteria
- **Certification level**: whether a specific certification is met (e.g., HIPAA, FISMA)
- **Audit pass rate**: proportion of internal audit requirements satisfied
- **Security vulnerability severity**: number of high-severity vulnerabilities

## Design Guidelines
- Understand the specialized requirements of the target industry
- Build compliance needs in from the earliest design stages
- Documentation and audit-trail capability

## Related Standards
- **Healthcare**: HIPAA, GDPR health data
- **Finance**: PCI DSS, GLBA, SOX
- **Education**: FERPA (student privacy)
- **Aviation**: DO-178C (avionics software)
